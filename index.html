I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Physics and Astronomy at the University of Louisville (UofL), advised by Prof. Benne Holwereda.

I started off my research in Stellar Astronomy where I dealt with modelling of starspots using TESS data. Then identified some Stellar popullations in the Magellanic Bridge. Currently, I am planning to incorporate Convolution Neural Network on the Euclid Q1 data and predict Star Formation Rate and Metallicity.

Before UofL, I completed my MSc in Physics & Astronomy at St. Xaviers, Mumbai, India in 2023. I enjoy Hiking, hiking and hiking.
